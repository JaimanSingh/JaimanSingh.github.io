# Haptix Support

Haptix is a click-wheel music player for your Apple Music. It runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later and needs the Music app on your iPhone.

## Contact

Email js.jaimansingh@gmail.com. Every message is read and answered.

## Common questions

**How do I use the wheel?** Slide your finger round the ring to move, press the centre to choose, press MENU at the top to go back, and press ••• for more actions.

**The screen is locked. How do I unlock it?** Turn the wheel once all the way round, in one direction, without stopping. Holding the centre locks it again.

**Haptix says Music access is off.** Open the Settings app, choose Haptix, and turn on Media & Apple Music.

**Songs from the Apple Music catalog don't play.** Playing catalog songs needs an Apple Music subscription. Your own library works without one.

**Does Haptix collect my data?** No. Read the [privacy policy](/haptix/privacy/).

Haptix is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Apple.
